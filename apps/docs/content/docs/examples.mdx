title: Examples
description: Four ways that the minimum change can be very different from “convert everything.”
---

## Already compatible: keep every byte

**Source:** PNG, 640 × 480, below 2 MB. **Target:** PNG, at most 2 MB.

All checks pass. Fitifact skips adaptation and offers the original file, with the same bytes and filename. Choose **Try an unchanged file** on the app’s starting screen.

## Image format change

**Source:** opaque PNG. **Target:** JPEG, at most 2 MB.

The format must change. The engine plans a JPEG encoding and validates its actual bytes and dimensions. Re-encoding removes metadata; quality may change. A transparent source is not silently flattened to satisfy a JPEG-only target.

## An explicit square crop

**Source:** 640 × 480 image. **Target:** exactly 480 × 480.

Fitting the original rectangle inside a square would not meet exact dimensions. The crop preview shows the removed edges and lets you adjust the position. Fitifact waits for your approval before discarding them.

## Remux a video

**Source:** MOV containing compatible H.264 video and AAC audio, within the byte limit. **Target:** MP4/H.264/AAC.

Only the container needs to change. The native provider can remux the streams to MP4 without re-encoding. The result is still inspected and validated. Available on desktop and CLI.

## Change only the incompatible stream

**Source:** MP4 with HEVC video and AAC audio. **Target:** MP4/H.264/AAC.

The video codec fails the target. The supported plan re-encodes video to H.264 and copies the compatible AAC soundtrack. Conversely, H.264 video with incompatible audio can keep the video and encode only audio.

Actual plans depend on inspected properties and confirmed constraints. A tighter byte ceiling may require more work than the examples above.
