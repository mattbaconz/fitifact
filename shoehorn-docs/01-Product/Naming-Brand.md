# Naming and brand

## Shoehorn is not cleared

As of 2026-08-15:
- **shoehorn.dev** is an intelligent developer platform;
- **@total-typescript/shoehorn** is an npm package.

Treat Shoehorn as a working codename until domain/package/trademark/searchability research is complete.

## Why metaphor works
A shoehorn helps something fit into a constrained destination, matching minimum-change adaptation.

## Risks
- implies forcing awkwardly;
- software collisions;
- `.dev` taken;
- package fragmentation.

## Final name criteria
1. 1–3 syllables;
2. easy to spell;
3. good CLI verb/noun;
4. evokes fit/compatibility;
5. no major collision;
6. package/domain feasibility;
7. broad consumer + developer fit;
8. not AI-specific.

## Semantic territories
- Fit: Mate, Seat, Slot, Snug
- Adapter: Coupler, Bridge, Jack, Dock
- Compliance: Pass, Conform, Satisfy
- Tailoring: Tailor, Hem
- Compatibility: Match, Accord

## Important
The YouTube title does not need the product name:
> **I Made an Adapter for Files**

So the project can rename without weakening launch.
